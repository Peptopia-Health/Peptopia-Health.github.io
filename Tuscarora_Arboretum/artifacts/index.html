Artifacts

The recovered-object collections of the Tuscarora Arboretum, organized by kind. Every entry is documented to the Arboretum standard — photographs, a short summary, the full study guide, and the audio brief. Bottles is the first collection open; Marbles and China follow as their first finds are documented.

Artifact Collections

Bottles

Machine-made and mouth-blown bottles from Martinsburg yards, privies, and ash pits — druggist ware, toilet bottles, and patent-medicine glass, each traced through glasshouse catalogs and the patent record.

View Bottles

Marbles

Clay, glass, and stone marbles — the small change of a century of Martinsburg childhoods, and surprisingly precise dating evidence. The first marble entries are being prepared.

China

Broken porcelain and earthenware — transferware shards, hotel china, and tableware fragments that date a yard as surely as any document. The first china entries are being prepared.
