The Galaxy Marble

GUIDE

Marble No. 1 · Glass — Marbles · Documented November 19, 2026 · Martinsburg, West Virginia

A pocket night sky: deep cobalt and black glass ribboned with brighter blue and flecked all through with white, like stars. Its surface carries the soft wear of real handling, and no maker’s mark — its maker and year stay an open question, studied honestly in the guide. Marble No. 1 opens the Arboretum’s marble collection, in the home state of Akro Agate, once the largest marble maker on Earth.

The Mint Swirl

GUIDE

Marble No. 2 · Glass — Marbles · Documented November 21, 2026 · Martinsburg, West Virginia

A swirl of mint and cream: pale green slag glass folded through milky white, one amber band at its edge, dark speckles across a frosted skin. Its glass matches the classic American slag marbles — Akro Agate’s Clarksburg greens above all — but it carries no maker’s mark, so its maker and year stay an open question, studied honestly in the guide. Marble No. 2 joins the Galaxy Marble in the Arboretum’s collection, in the home state of Akro Agate, once the largest marble maker on Earth.
