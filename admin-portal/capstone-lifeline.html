🎓 RNTTP Capstone — Project Lifeline

Artificial Intelligence and the Future of Veteran Care
David Carroll, RN · RN Transition to Practice Program · Martinsburg VA Medical Center
The thesis: AI will transform health care within five years. The VA — the largest integrated health system in America — can lead that change or be dragged by it. This capstone proposes five concrete improvements, the guardrails that make them safe, and the steps to be ready before the future arrives.

The Lifeline — Five Phases

1
Now – Dec 2026 · Foundation

Build the base

Kickoff briefing and this lifeline guide published. Research file opens: AI in documentation, medication safety, navigation, staffing, and training — one topic at a time, with sources.

2
Jan – Mar 2027 · Evidence

Gather the proof

A personal friction log: every workflow that wastes nursing time or risks safety, in general terms, never with patient information. Three entries a week. RNTTP coordinator confirms the capstone format early, so the build targets the real thing.

3
Apr – May 2027 · Proposal

Sharpen the five improvements

Each proposal gets the same four parts: what it is, why it matters to veterans, the first concrete step, and how we will know it worked. Guardrails written in full.

4
Jun – Aug 2027 · Build

Make the presentation

Slides, illustrations, and audio built and rehearsed until it runs like a story, not a lecture. Muse is the build partner throughout — research, drafts, visuals, practice runs.

5
Sep 2027 · Delivery

Present the capstone

The case to the RNTTP class and VA leadership: the future is coming within five years, here is what it can do for our veterans, and here is how we start now.

The Five Proposals

Documentation Partner

Ambient AI drafts the note from the real conversation, so the chart stops competing with the patient.

Medication Safety Co-Pilot

An intelligent second check at the point of administration — med-pass as a verified system, not a memory test.

Veteran Navigation Agent

One trusted guide for appointments, refills, and benefits, in plain language, at any hour.

Predictive Staffing & Flow

Systems that see the surge coming, so nurse managers plan instead of scramble.

AI Literacy for Every Nurse

A curriculum piloted through RNTTP itself — a workforce that understands the tools and leads them.

The Guardrails

Human in the loop Privacy first Checked for bias Never automate the relationship Nurses at the design table
