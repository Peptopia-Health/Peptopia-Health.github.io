AI and the Future of Veteran Care — how artificial intelligence can transform the care the VA provides veterans, and how the VA should prepare now for the Singularity, expected within five years. A one-year project, November 2026 to September 2027.

The Argument

The VA is an antiquated, inefficient bureaucracy — and it cares for the veterans who carried this country. Artificial intelligence can greatly improve that care today: shorter notes, safer med passes, easier navigation, smarter staffing. And because the Singularity is coming within five years, the time to prepare is now — with nurses at the design table, not watching from the hallway.

The Five Proposals

1

Documentation Partner

AI drafts the note while the nurse cares for the patient. The nurse reviews, edits, and signs — time returns to the bedside.

2

Medication Safety Co-Pilot

A second set of eyes on every med pass — scans, rights, interactions — flagging trouble before a medication is given.

3

Veteran Navigation Agent

One guide through the maze: benefits, appointments, records, and answers a veteran can actually reach.

4

Predictive Staffing & Flow

Forecast census and acuity so units are staffed before the surge — not scrambling after it.

5

AI Literacy for Every Nurse

Every nurse trained to use, question, and oversee AI — piloted right here in RNTTP.
