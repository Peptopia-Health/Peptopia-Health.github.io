7% Extract Dose vs. Leaf Powder Equivalent

Comparing WestKoast 7% extract dose (g) to standard 1.5% leaf powder equivalent (g).

How it works: leaf equivalent = extract dose × 7 ÷ 1.5 (about 4.67×). So 1.0 g of 7% extract ≈ 4.67 g of standard 1.5% leaf.
