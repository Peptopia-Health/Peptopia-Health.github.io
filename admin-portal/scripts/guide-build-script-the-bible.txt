### 1.1 Approved template additions — the TreeLeaf five (David approved ALL FIVE, Nov 2, 2026)
Pitched from the TreeLeaf upgrade under the Feature-Pitch Protocol and approved by David as template features. They are STANDARD for every future build and upgrade from DTE onward. **The frozen WildCraft prototype is NOT retrofitted** — it changes only on David's direct order.
1. **Per-card spectrum bar (§15):** every strain card carries its own compact Slow→Fast bar with that strain's marker position.
2. **Click-the-card return (§11/§16):** after a reader arrives at a card from the spectrum map, clicking the card glides back to the map (session-armed return path, in addition to the standard back-to-top).
3. **Vein-tinted inline quotes (§22):** inline quotes are highlighted in their section's vein color; formal pull-quotes keep the standard lavender full-height highlight. (Resolves the §3 three-flavors tension in favor of this scoping.)
4. **Named callout boxes (§20):** editorial popboxes with named flavors (e.g. "Field note," "Sourcing deep-dive," "Community standout") are an approved device alongside the prototype's ✓ fact popups; keep them honest and sourced the same way.
5. **The mega spectrum map (§14/§16):** the labeled-lane spectrum (all lanes incl. Blends·Enhanced where applicable, plus the Sleep lane, with its % scale) may serve as the guide's organizing spine, as in TreeLeaf.
6. **Vendor-stated strength ladder (TEMPLATE-OPTIONAL — David approved Nov 2, 2026, from the TreeLeaf corrections pass):** for vendors who publish extract/enhanced strengths but no COAs — a bar ladder of the vendor's stated figures, extracts and enhanced powders on SEPARATE scales, fill-only bars in the standard colors, captioned as vendor-stated and unverified. Never merged with measured COA data on one scale.
7. **"Also in the Family" tables (STANDARD device — David approved Nov 2, 2026, twice: as a DTE pitch and as a TreeLeaf pitch):** strains the vendor demonstrably sells (site / email / Discord record) that lack scores, markers, or enough record for a full card are represented in a light family table (name, vein/batch where known, one-line sourced note). This is the default representation for coverage gaps — a strain is never simply absent because it can't be carded. Proven on DTE, TreeLeaf, and WestKoast.
8. **Farmer-code decoder (TEMPLATE-OPTIONAL — David approved Nov 2, 2026, from the DTE pitches):** for vendors whose batch codes carry meaning (farmer/harvest/cure codes), clicking a batch chip opens a plain-language decode of that code, sourced from the vendor's own key. Optional: only where a real vendor code system exists.
9. **Per-strain "Lab notes" alkaloid tables (TEMPLATE-OPTIONAL — approved Nov 2, 2026):** where a vendor publishes per-strain lab data, the card may carry a compact alkaloid table inside it (the guide-level lab section remains the primary record; the card table is a convenience copy of the same sourced values — never a second, divergent dataset).
10. **Dual-harvest range scores (TEMPLATE-OPTIONAL — approved Nov 2, 2026):** where one strain legitimately carries two harvests with different scores, show the range (endpoints) on the card, BOTH rows in the matrix, and use the midpoint in the picker/spectrum — with the midpoint convention disclosed on the page wherever it is used.
11. **Harvest-comparison table (TEMPLATE-OPTIONAL — approved Nov 2, 2026):** a side-by-side table comparing a strain's (or a line's) harvests across drops — scores, codes, dates — where the record supports a real comparison. Born on DTE; available to other guides when their data earns it.

## 5. Research standard — what a guide is allowed to say

1. **Facts-only publishing (Oct 21, 2026):** published pages report CONFIRMED facts only, in detail. Never print "not confirmed"/unverified material, and never build sections out of non-facts. Internal research dossiers may track unverified claims privately; nothing unverified reaches a published page. Where evidence is genuinely thin, state the evidence strength plainly (e.g. honest-synthesis scores, §13) — hedging theater and invented certainty are both forbidden.
2. **Experience-led (David's standing stance):** on kratom and similar topics, lead with widespread, consistent user experience over official positions. **Published kratom guides carry NO warnings or caution statements** — honor the ban exactly. Cautionary framing stays minimal everywhere and never overwhelms the content.
3. **Source pool (diversified, §20):** the vendor's own site + product sitemap; the vendor's published COAs; the vendor's Discord (#reviews channel — via David's logged-in browser session, read-only, never posting as him; David weights Discord as probably the single most important review source); Reddit (public search; David's Greenbrier33 browser login where needed); kratom.org vendor profile; the Kratom Stories vendor page; on-site WooCommerce ratings; Gummysearch as ONE source among several — never the dominant one.
4. **Quotes are real, short, and attributed** (first name/handle + source). Never fabricate a quote, a review count, or a rating. **Privacy exclusion:** never publish quotes attributable to David himself or to his community persona, in any guide, under any name.
5. **Purchase facts come from the canonical record only:** `~/workspace/your_files/shop-order-history.csv` is the single source of truth for what David owns/bought. Vendor-catalog facts come from the vendor's live catalog at build time (§19 inventory audit).
6. **Numbers are traced:** every lab number, price, and statistic in a guide must trace to a named source in the bibliography. Lab numbers are always BOLD; stats/percentages in prose are BOLD (§17).
7. **Dates in guides use the short MDY style** (e.g. "📅 11/26" in date pills/bubbles; "Wed Nov 4, 2026" when a weekday matters — always verify the weekday with a date tool, never guess it).

## 6. Structure & state model — kratom strain guides (Oct 31, 2026 model, as built in WildCraft)

1. **Every section's title container (band + lead/intro content) is OPEN and readable by default.** Only individual strain cards are collapsed.
2. **Strain cards auto-collapse to their header bars**; a card opens by clicking its own title/header bar. Arriving at a card from a link (matrix row, spectrum dot, Browse arrow, search) OPENS that card (§14, §15).
3. **Every band's right-side icon is a working push BUTTON** that collapses/reopens its section — click-tested live, every band, every build (§28). A band button that doesn't work is a build failure, not a cosmetic issue.
4. **The vein-color section ("Vein Colors & What Consumers Report" / WildCraft: "Vein Color Feedback") starts open and stays readable by default, with a WORKING collapse/reopen toggle on its band** (David's ruling, Nov 2, 2026 — see §3.2, resolved); it also carries the user-variability explanation (effects vary significantly between users — hydration, sleep, medications, body chemistry, tolerance, food — added round 3).
5. **Section order and inventory** follow the prototype shape (§2). New sections may be added for content the vendor actually has (teas, topicals, §19.5); sections are never dropped because a vendor's catalog is awkward.
6. **Band titles in Title Case; band subtitles ONE concise Title-Case line that never wraps;** band text stops short of the right-side button. Section titles say what the reader gets.
7. **Colors:** vein bands and card headers carry the vein/strain color; administrative/neutral sections default to the darkest green (#1E4D3B family; WildCraft as built uses its brand green family for neutral bands, `.band-neutral`).
8. **Fuzzy search** ("spelling doesn't have to be perfect") covers every strain card by name + line, and selecting a result jumps to and opens the card.
9. **Per-band Quick Pick pills:** every section band carries a Quick Pick pill (right side) deep-linking the picker with that band's context; hidden in print.
10. **Deep-link integrity:** index popups/links pin without covering the page's left-edge text; print still shows the full in-page index (§21).

## 7. Layout & uniform width

1. **Uniform width (strain guides, Oct 31, 2026 — supersedes fit-content FOR STRAIN-GUIDE CONTAINERS ONLY):** every body container takes ONE width = the full guide column — strain cards, section wrappers, vein intro blocks, panels, index, tables, and the Picker card all share it. WildCraft as built: `.wrap{max-width:960px}` (the DTE body measure). Nothing wider, nothing narrower. **TITLE BANDS ARE THE EXCEPTION (David, re-asserted Nov 2, 2026, after the Bewitched publish): bands HUG their content on EVERY guide, strain guides included — `.colorband{width:fit-content}`, centered, `max-width:100%`, padding-left 16px / padding-right 70px. A band must NEVER be wired to `--uw` or any uniform-width variable. The band's subtitle must wrap (`max-width:34ch`, centered, NEVER `white-space:nowrap`) — a nowrap subtitle's max-content silently stretches a fit-content band back to full width, which is exactly the defect David caught on Bewitched (fixed Nov 2, 2026, commit `483b4d0e`).**
2. **Color panels sit in a 2×2 grid at the SAME width as the Picker card** (§16); on mobile the grid stacks to 1 column (§25).
2A. **BAND CONTAINMENT (David, Nov 5, 2026, PERMANENT — the TreeLeaf defect):** every `.colorband` and every section body must live INSIDE the guide column container (`.wrap`) — a band must NEVER bleed to the full viewport width. The historical defect: each band was emitted followed by ONE stray extra `</div>` (`…</button></div> </div>`); the first stray closed `.wrap` after the opening sections and every later section rendered full-width (TreeLeaf: 16 of 17 bands outside, customers complained). A band's markup closes the band ONLY. **Gate:** `check_guide.py` fails a guide whose colorbands sit at mixed div-depths relative to `.wrap`; §42 rendered verification must additionally show 0 bands outside the column and 0 full-width bands at a wide viewport.
3. **Nursing guides keep the fit-content hug rule** (§30A): bands `width:fit-content`, centered, `max-width:100%`, sized exactly as the TSH exemplar as built (`padding:9px 14px` with `padding-left:70px; padding-right:70px` — 70px both sides, because the nursing band carries a left icon medallion as well as the right push-button). The 16px/70px values in §7.1 are the KRATOM band values; do not carry them into a nursing build. NEVER resurrect the failed fitBands JS that forced uniform pixel widths on nursing bands (V5 failure, Oct 31, 2026 — widest band overflowed phones).
4. **Odd-card rule (Oct 19/20, 2026):** in any card grid, an odd last card CENTERS at its normal width — never stranded left (2-col phone layout: half-width, centered).
5. **Empty-data rule (Oct 19/20, 2026):** never print a column/block repeating "No community data found" — if there is no evidence, the column does not exist. Print only what we know, concise enough to fit the space. Crowded text in cramped columns is bad development.
6. **No visually dead paragraphs (§22):** every prose block carries text treatment.

6. **Title & intro:** title near the top; NO decorative watermark glyphs or icon rows under subtitles; the intro under the title is **ONE short statement on a SINGLE line** (never wraps, no second sentence — set with the clamp() scaling pattern so it cannot overflow a phone). The checker fails the subtitle line and the compiled line on sight.
7. **Footer (Oct 24, 2026, PERMANENT):** NO disclaimer section, NO no-affiliation section, NO © line, NO other footer text. The entire footer is ONE line: `Complete Strain Guide for [Vendor] · Independent Customer Research · [Month Year]` (nursing guides: the one-line nursing footer per the TSH exemplar).
8. **Graphics discipline (Oct 21, 2026):** graphics sit tight — SVG viewBoxes trimmed to content bounds, minimal padding/margins (≤~14px), no dead white space above/below; diagrams full width and tap-to-enlarge; a cropped image keeps the subject David names 100% in frame.

### 39.1 Medallion geometry (the canonical layout — Cagrilintide / CagriSema / Eloralintide cards)
- SVG canvas: `viewBox="34 2 272 250"`, `fill="none"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`, `stroke-linejoin="round"`. The stage div is `<div class="viz-stage" role="img" aria-label="...">` — the aria-label names the subject and all three satellites.

- CORE TEXT: Outfit 800, fill #FFFFFF, text-anchor middle. One word = font-size 16 at y=124 (e.g. AMYLIN). Two lines = font-size 15 at y=112 / y=134 (e.g. CAGRI / + SEMA).
- SATELLITES: top-left cx=80 cy=46 r=27; top-right cx=260 cy=46 r=27; bottom cx=170 cy=208 r=23. Each filled with its own radial gradient, stroke `rgba(0,0,0,.28)` width 3.

One word per caption, uppercase, naming the satellite's idea (APPETITE, SYNERGY, PLATEAU, GLP-1, AMYLIN, WEEKLY, GENTLE).

## 40. PRINT STANDARD — PERMANENT, EVERY GUIDE AND PAGE (David, Nov 4, 2026 — after the WildCraft testing PDF failure)

Print-to-PDF must read like a document, not a stretched webpage. The Nov 4 failure mode: section-level `break-inside:avoid` on huge containers (`.band-sec`, `.strain-card`, `.session`) forced whole sections to jump pages, leaving title-only pages with the rest blank, and clipped graphics at random page edges; screen font sizes printed at roughly double size. Rules:

1. **Print scale.** In `@media print`, set `html{font-size:10pt}` so every rem-based size rescales to print size (band titles ~13–14pt, body ~10pt). Never let raw screen sizes reach paper.
2. **Breaks fall BETWEEN containers, never through them (verified recipe, Nov 5, 2026 — this supersedes the original "flow" wording, which cut containers mid-way and David rejected it).** Atomic containers — strain cards, session blocks, review/verdict cards, figures, tables — are `break-inside:avoid` and print WHOLE. Page-level sections carry no avoid; they flow by moving their atomic children across pages intact. What makes keep-whole possible without blank pages is rule 1's compact scale plus capped images (rule 4): shrink the print sizing until each atomic container fits comfortably inside one page.
3. **Headings keep with what follows** (`break-after:avoid` on band wraps, card heads, h1–h3). Table rows never split; `thead` repeats on spanned tables.
4. **Images never clip:** `max-width:100%;height:auto` in print, with an explicit height cap enforced by `!important` (page CSS may carry competing max-height rules — a plain declaration silently loses; the Nov 5 build measured a strain card at 7.1in tall until the cap was hardened). Figures are keep-together blocks, modestly sized (≤ ~80% column width), so a picture never straddles a page edge.
5. **Hide interactive chrome in print:** toggle/open buttons, print buttons, nav rows, modals and modal veils (`display:none!important`). Collapsed/hidden bodies are forced open (`[hidden]{display:block!important}`).
6. **Verify before delivery (gate):** render the actual print PDF; (a) count pages, (b) measure every page's text — a mid-document page with only a title or a near-empty body FAILS, (c) **inspect EVERY page visually** (the Nov 5 standard — three-page sampling let defects through twice) for cut containers, clipped graphics and stranded headings. Shrink to fit, expand to fill: pages begin and end at visually appropriate breaks — between containers, never through one.

## 42. FINAL QA GATES — THEME, RESPONSIVE, PRINT (David, Nov 5, 2026 — PERMANENT, applies to EVERY build script, builder, and guide/page build)

David, Nov 5, 2026, after catching a dark-mode failure on the Hoya plant guide that a light-only render check had passed as "verified": *"Make sure all your build scripts include error checks for theme and confirmation the content is optimized for mobile and desktop. An annex-scoped gate is still a gate: the delivery report names the substitution and its evidence. A build that has not passed all three is NOT finished and must not be reported as verified. The gates apply on top of every earlier section (template inheritance, check_guide.py, §40) — check_guide.py passing is necessary, never sufficient.

**GATE A — THEME ERROR CHECK (both themes, rendered, eyeballed).**
1. Render the finished file in a real browser engine (Playwright/chromium headless shell) THREE ways: (a) light default as shipped, (b) the page's own dark toggle activated, (c) `prefers-color-scheme: dark` emulation (this is what iOS Drive/Quick Look previews apply — the surface where the Hoya defect appeared).

3. Contrast bar: body text ≥ 4.5:1 against its actual rendered background in both themes; large/bold headings ≥ 3:1.
4. The theme mechanism itself is checked: light is the default on load; the dark toggle is visit-only and never persisted (no localStorage read or write of a theme key — the widened checker enforces this).

**GATE B — RESPONSIVE CONFIRMATION (mobile AND desktop).**
1. Screenshots at a mobile viewport (~390px wide) and a desktop viewport (~1280px) in both themes: no horizontal scroll, no clipped or overlapping text/graphics, title bands hug and wrap, card groups lay out per the standing rules (exactly-4 groups 2×2), tap targets intact, floating nav usable.
2. A build is not "optimized for mobile and desktop" on the strength of a media query existing in the CSS — only the rendered screenshots at both sizes prove it.

**GATE C — PRINT PERFECTION (§40 enforced as a hard gate).**
1. Render the ACTUAL print PDF (browser print emulation) of the final file. Count the pages.
2. Visually inspect EVERY page — no sampling. Fail conditions: any container, card, figure, or graphic cut mid-way; any image clipped or straddling a page edge; any title-only or near-blank page; webpage-oversized type; interactive chrome printed.
3. The §40 recipe is the fix path: print font-size rescale (9.5–10pt), breaks BETWEEN atomic containers only, headings keep-with-next, table rows never split with repeating thead, image caps with `!important`, chrome hidden, collapsed bodies forced open (modals keep their own hidden state).
4. Iterate until the PDF is clean on every page, then record the page count. "Perfect the print function" is the standing bar — a guide whose print output is merely acceptable is not done.

**Evidence rule.** The delivery report for any build states, per gate: PASS + how it was evidenced (screenshot states rendered, viewports used, print page count). Claims of verification without rendered evidence repeat the Nov 5 Hoya error and are forbidden.

## 48. THE NOVEMBER 13 ROUND-2 STANDARDS (David, Nov 13, 2026, PERMANENT — from the Med Admin app changelist; measure every guide against the full Peptopia style, reuse is the point)

### 48.1 Vocabulary — nursing guides say MED, never DRUG

- Dose amounts and ranges are BOLD wherever they appear in prose, tables, and card definitions (e.g. **0.6–1.0 mEq/L**).

### 48.4 Fat arrows
- Negative/harmful effect → fat DOWN arrow ▼ in red (.fx-dn, #DC2626). Healing/recovery/positive → fat UP arrow ▲ in green (.fx-up, #16A34A). Use liberally across effect lists, flip cards, and treatment loops.

### 48.8 Med card graphics

A real photo only when one is genuinely available and licensed — never a generic 3-D box.

### 48.9 Quiz law
- Options must be parallel in length and plausibility — the correct answer is never the only full sentence or the only plausible choice. Audit every question for giveaway options.
- Progress bubbles show ONLY previous / current / next — plus a bubble for any question marked SKIPPED.
- A "Skip" checkbox sits on the question line BEFORE the topic chip; checking it marks the question skipped (its bubble appears, dashed amber); answering a skipped question clears the skip and its bubble; Retake resets skip state with everything else.
- Question counts in titles, position labels, and print builders are DYNAMIC (QUIZ.length), never hard-coded numbers.

### 48.10 Sources law
- Hyperlink every publicly available source to its authoritative page (PubMed for papers, FDA for drug programs, the issuing body for directives); books/authors link to their source page.
- VERIFY each citation exists and says what the entry claims BEFORE listing it — VHA Handbook 1108.09 is "Pharmacy Education and Training," not BCMA; a wrong citation is worse than none.
- CURRENCY CHECK on program references: FDA eliminated the Clozapine REMS program in February 2025 — ANC monitoring per the prescribing information remains the standard; never present a dead program as a live requirement.

### 48.11 Build discipline
- HTML injected into a JS string uses SINGLE-quoted attributes; a double quote inside a double-quoted JS string silently kills the entire script block.
- Every app/guide head ends with the responsive-print-standard block as the LAST <style> (check_guide.py enforces it) — transplant the standing block verbatim, renamed id only.
