Full Guide: [topic]

Builds a complete Peptopia guide in the house standard — research, build, audio summary in chat, published on your approval.

  1. Say the phrase in chat, with your topic after the colon.
  2. Researched thoroughly — primary sources, community experience, real numbers.
  3. Built to the full house standard — every section, graphic, and table; must pass the build checker.
  4. Findings plus an audio summary in chat — short, medium, or extended, your pick.
  5. Publishes live on your approval; the page is verified.

Mini Guide: [topic]

One subject on one compact page — Short-n-Sweet, ready to print.

  1. Say the phrase with one subject — or hand over your own steps for a how-to.
  2. Subject mode: the subject researched, nothing padded. Steps mode: your steps cleaned and distilled, nothing added.
  3. Photo up top; facts as facts, inferences labeled, references linked — or numbered step cards naming each app switch.
  4. Must pass the build checker, like every guide.
  5. Delivered in chat — one clean page, ready to print; copy filed in the matching Drive folder.

Plant Guide: [name]

One-page plant guide published in the Tuscarora Plants section; files uploaded to Drive.

  1. Say it in chat — or Author → 57 → Arboretum / Plants on the Arboretum site, up to 4 photos.
  2. Researched: accepted name + synonym, family, origin, care numbers, propagation, pests, toxicity / pet safety.
  3. One-page visual guide — leaf anatomy, care meters, care card, propagation steps, troubleshooting table.
  4. Publishes in Tuscarora Plants, with its own card on the Plants page.
  5. Filed in Drive (Tuscarora Arboretum › Plants) — file + live link delivered in chat.

Example. Type in your plant name and then copy to clipboard.

Artifact: [brief]

A full artifact package — storytelling guide with timeline, photos, short audio, patent links and references.

  1. Say the phrase with a short brief — or Author → 57 → Arboretum / Bottles, with photos.
  2. Researched: age, origin, maker, circulation — facts as facts, inferences labeled.
  3. Storytelling guide: timeline graphic + best photos; a found patent’s popup + link woven in.
  4. Short fireside audio brief (about two minutes), in the Smooth voice.
  5. Publishes on the Tuscarora Arboretum — own card, own page.

Icon: [subject]

One icon in the toolbox style — your subject drawn in neon lines on a see-through glass disc. The phrase is the whole brief; nothing else to explain.

  1. Type Icon: and your subject — any subject at all.
  2. The subject is drawn large in neon lines inside a round glass disc, glowing neon border at the edge — the approved toolbox icon's exact construction.
  3. The picture is shown to you on black and on white, so you can judge it as it will really look.
  4. On your one-word approval, the picture file is saved into the Icon Masters store.
  5. The new icon is then used wherever that subject appears on the site.
