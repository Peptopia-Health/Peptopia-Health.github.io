How to read the flags in this briefing

  • Confirmed Read live on the vendor’s own site Nov 9, 2026, or from a primary PDF / newsletter.
  • Single-source Rests on one synthesis or aggregator. Keep the hedge when you copy it into a guide — don’t upgrade it to fact.
  • Vendor claim Stated by the vendor (often in a newsletter or menu) with no COA or independent document sighted.
  • Not located Searched for and not found in this research. That is a bounded negative finding, not proof it doesn’t exist in unindexed channels.

Community sentiment for all three skews positive in the public record found. The main criticisms for DTE and WildCraft are operational — sell-outs, drop delays, price — not safety. No negative-review corpus was found for Tree Leaf.
