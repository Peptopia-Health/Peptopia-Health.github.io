NOV 26Federal

Federal 7-OH scheduling still unpublished; court fight may push it into 2027

The temporary federal scheduling rule for 7-OH has still not been published. In the American Kratom Association’s D.C. lawsuit, Justice Department attorneys attributed the delay to ongoing consultations with the Department of Health and Human Services and asked for a stay; a status report is due December 14, with briefing running through January 13, so the schedule could slip into early 2027. Mitragynine products covered by the August action (MP / MGM-15 / MGM-16) remain Schedule I.

Source: Pain News Network, Nov 25, 2026

NOV 25Protective

Gloversville, New York tables its kratom ban

The Common Council tabled Intro LL-2 on November 24, the night of its own public hearing. The proposal would have banned the sale and possession-for-sale of kratom, natural leaf included; tabling means it was not adopted.

Source: Hudson Valley Botanicals hearing tracker

NOV 24Restriction

New York signs 7-OH limits into law

Governor Hochul signed Chapter 415 (S.8925-A / A.9156-B) on November 23. The law prohibits products with 7-OH over 2% of total alkaloids or over 1 mg per serving, with civil fines of $500–$1,000, and takes effect February 21, 2027. Kratom otherwise stays legal in New York at 21+.

Source: Office of the Governor announcement

NOV 24Process

Whatcom County, Washington weighs 21+ rules against a full ban

The County Council discussed competing ordinances on November 24 and adopted nothing. One draft regulates sales at 21+ with signage and a $250 fine; a sheriff’s version adds ID checks for anyone appearing under 30; a third would ban commercial kratom activity in unincorporated county areas while expressly preserving personal use. A December 8 hearing has been reported but is not confirmed scheduled — and Bellingham, Blaine and Lynden, all inside the county, have already banned sales.

Source: Cascadia Daily News, Nov 24, 2026

NOV 23Process

Florida’s emergency labeling rule takes effect

Emergency Rule 5KER26-16 is in force from November 23, 2026 through February 21, 2027. It requires disclosure of 7-OH concentration and alkaloid ratio on kratom products. Florida remains a regulated 21+ state.

Source: Florida Administrative Register

NOV 20Restriction

Pocatello, Idaho bans commercial sales citywide

The City Council voted November 19 to ban commercial kratom sales inside city limits; personal possession remains legal. Idaho has no statewide kratom law, so city rules decide.

Source: Local News 8 / KIFI reporting

NOV 20Restriction

Warren County, New York bans all kratom sales

The Board of Supervisors voted unanimously on November 20 to adopt Local Law 9 of 2026, prohibiting the sale and distribution of kratom in all forms, natural leaf included, with fines up to $1,000. Personal possession is not banned.

Source: Warren County announcement

NOV 20Process

Montana files a Schedule I ban bill for 2027

SB 32 (Sen. Mark Noland), introduced November 20 for the 2027 session, would add mitragynine and hydroxymitragynine to Schedule I. Montana has no statewide kratom law today; a KCPA bill was tabled in committee in September for not being strict enough.

Source: Montana Legislature Bill Explorer
