What the crawl surfaced

  • The homepage is a dead end by design — holder page, zero outbound links. The two hubs it should hang from (Admin Portal, X-Peps Research Hub) are themselves orphans: nothing links to them either.
  • Duplicates are everywhere — the same guide at two URLs: DTE (×2 plus a short guide), WestKoast (×2), WildCraft (×2 plus two testing copies), DSIP (×2), Kratom Potentiators (×2), Corrections Mental Health (×2), Aftership (×3, one nested at /X-Peps/X-Peps/). In each pair, typically only one twin is linked; the other is an orphan.
