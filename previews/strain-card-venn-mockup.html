Strain Card Graphic — Mockup

A new graphic row for strain review cards. The strain’s color wheel moves to the left of its container, and a new Effect Venn sits to its right — one translucent circle for each effect the strain offers, filled with that effect’s palette color, labeled with its score.

The Venn rules (David, Nov 26, 2026): one circle per effect in the strain’s own profile — the same effects its color wheel shows — and nothing else; and the Venn is drawn at exactly the color wheel’s size, its twin. Premium Select — Red Vein carries four (Calm · Pain · Mood · Energy), so its Venn has four circles. Premium Select — White Vein carries four (Energy · Mood · Calm · Pain), so its Venn has four. The Venn grows and shrinks with the strain.
